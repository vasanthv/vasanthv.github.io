I am not a photographer by any means, but every once in a while, I look at a frame of what I am seeing and think, "Oh, this would make a good photo." And, to be honest, most of the time, it turns out to be mediocre.

A few years ago, I used to share those photos on Instagram. Now that I've ditched all my social media presence, and this blog is the only place where I share things, I needed a way to share photos here.

My immediate thought was to create a photo tag and use that, so all the photos could be filtered by the tag and become part of the blog feed too. But I also like the wall of photos you see on Instagram. So I decided I needed nothing less than that and created a `/photos` page powered by the photo tag.

So, here I am: introducing <a href="/photos">/photos</a>, with some random shots I've taken over the past couple of months.
